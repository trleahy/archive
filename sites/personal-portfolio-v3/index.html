👋🏼 I’m Raff, a Graphics & Playout Operator for QVC UK (part of the QVC Group)

As a Graphics & Playout Operator, I play a crucial role in delivering captivating visual content for live broadcasts. I am also responsible for asset management, monitoring and playout. Providing stills, VTs and other assets for use by the director.
